Inbound calls

Picking up for you, qualifying, triggering the action

When a customer calls, the voice agent answers instantly, understands the request and handles it through to the end — and only hands over to a human when it's really needed.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. Detection & understanding. The agent recognizes the voice, identifies the reason for the call (appointment, quote, support, information) and the contact in your CRM if one exists.
  2. Live action. It opens your calendar, your CRM, your mailbox — and performs the task during the conversation: creating an opportunity, booking an appointment, sending a quote.
  3. Record & follow-up. Everything is logged: full transcript, updated CRM record, confirmation email/SMS sent to the customer, alert to your team if needed.

Real-world use cases

  • Appointment scheduling (viewing, consultation, service call) with live slot selection.
  • Qualifying an inbound request before transferring it to the right person.
  • Creating an express quote for a standardized service, emailed during the call.
  • Customer information (opening hours, prices, FAQ) with a tone and style consistent with your brand.
  • Order or case tracking from your CRM and your management tool.
  • Logging a complaint, opening a ticket and notifying the customer support team.

Outbound calls

Prospecting, follow-up and retention at scale

The agent calls your lists, introduces itself with your script, qualifies the opportunity and triggers the next step — an email, an appointment or a scheduled follow-up.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. Preparation. You upload the contact list (CSV export, CRM or enriched source) and choose the scenario: prospecting, follow-up, re-engagement, appointment setting.
  2. Execution. The agent makes the calls in waves, handles first-level objections, qualifies against your criteria and tags each contact in your CRM.
  3. Follow-up loop. Unanswered calls and undecided contacts move into a multichannel cadence (callback, email, SMS) until the deal is closed or they are disqualified.

Real-world use cases

  • Cold prospecting on an industry list to land qualified appointments.
  • Following up dormant leads after X days of inactivity in the CRM.
  • Confirming appointments the day before (viewing, service call, consultation).
  • Post-service satisfaction survey, with scoring and alerts on negative feedback.
  • Re-engaging former customers on a new offer or a pricing change.
  • Verifying contact details or needs before sending a human sales rep.

Workflow 01 · Recruitment

Automated candidate screening

The agent calls or calls back every candidate received, asks the pre-qualification questions and sorts the pipeline before a human spends a single minute on it.

Workflow steps

  1. An application comes in (form, email, job board) → contact created.
  2. Outbound call or appointment booking for a 5–10 min pre-qualification conversation.
  3. Questions on experience, availability, salary expectations, motivations — answers scored.
  4. Automatic sorting: to interview / on hold / polite rejection (with a standardized email).
  5. Summary sent to the recruiter, in-person interview booked for shortlisted profiles.

Business benefit

  • The recruiter only sees candidates who have already been screened.
  • Every candidate gets a reply, which protects your employer brand.
  • Time to first contact cut from several days to a few hours.

Who it's for

SMEs and firms that recruit continuously for high-volume roles (field, support, sales).

Workflow 04 · CRM data

Continuous CRM enrichment and hygiene

A clean CRM is rarely the result of human discipline — it is the result of an agent that cleans it continuously.

Workflow steps

  1. Detection of contacts that are incomplete, duplicated or inactive for X months.
  2. Automatic enrichment of missing fields (job title, industry, company size).

Business benefit

  • Sales decisions based on reliable data, not a fuzzy CRM.
  • Sales time saved on contacts that would never have converted.

Who it's for

Workflow 05 · Campaigns

Scheduled, segmented SMS and email campaigns

You decide the message and the target. The agent segments, personalizes, sends at the right time and measures.

Workflow steps

  1. Segment definition (recent customers, dormant customers, by industry, by average basket).
  2. Message personalization: first name, last interaction, tailored offer.
  3. Orchestrated sending at the best times, respecting contact frequency limits.
  4. Live tracking: opens, clicks, replies, opt-outs.
  5. Handover to a call from the agent for the most engaged contacts.

Business benefit

  • No more campaigns sent without follow-up: every open can trigger an action.
  • Unsubscribe rate kept under control by capping the number of sends.
  • Unified view of channels: call + email + SMS in the same sequence.

Who it's for

B2C or B2B companies with a base of at least a few hundred contacts who want to reactivate the value they have already earned.
